3

File your claim

Your first move depends on where you are.

Still serving? Protect the 180-to-90-days-before-separation window. Filing inside it — the BDD program — is what gets a decision close to your discharge date, and once it passes you cannot get it back. Work backwards from your separation date and set a reminder at 180 days. Inside 90 days you can still file, just not through BDD.

Already out? Start the online claim today, even with nothing gathered. VA sets your effective date the moment you begin the online form — before you submit it — and back pay runs from that date. Records can follow over the next few weeks without costing you anything.

Filing on paper instead? Submit an Intent to File (21-0966) first; that is what preserves the date for paper claims. Online filers do not need it.

The forms, and what each is for

Form numbers are how VA talks about all of this. Worth knowing which is which.

FormWhat it's for
DD 2807-1 Report of Medical History. Your account of every condition, with an explanation for each "Yes" in Item 29. Prefill it →
SHA Part A Separation Health Assessment self-assessment, completed as you leave service. Prefill it →
21-0966 Intent to File. Preserves your effective date on a paper claim. Filing online does this for you. VA →
21-526EZ The disability compensation claim itself. VA →
21-4142 Authorizes VA to request your private (non-VA) medical records. VA →
21-10210 Lay / witness statement — the official "buddy letter" form. VA →
21-4138 Statement in Support of Claim. Your own narrative, in your words. VA →
Buddy letters carry real weight. A statement from someone who watched you change — a spouse, roommate, supervisor — can establish things a medical record never captured. The tool above generates a template per condition, pre-filled with what your record documents so the writer doesn't contradict it by accident.

"Presumptive" is worth understanding early. For some conditions tied to a time, place or exposure, VA presumes the service connection instead of making you prove it. If one of yours qualifies, it changes what evidence you need. Your VSO will know which apply to you.
