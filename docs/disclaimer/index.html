Before you start

Four things worth thirty seconds, so you know exactly what this is and what happens to your records.

This is not a VA website, and it does not file anything.

It is an independent tool with no connection to the Department of Veterans Affairs or the Department of Defense. Nothing here is submitted to VA. It does not advise you on what to claim or what you are owed — that is an accredited Veterans Service Officer’s job, and they are free.

What it does: reads your own health records, fills in as much of DD Form 2807-1 and the Separation Health Assessment as the evidence honestly supports, and hands you a package to take to that appointment.

Your records never leave your computer.

The tool runs entirely inside your browser tab. There is no account, no upload, and no server to send anything to. You can disconnect from the internet once the page has loaded and it still works.

Progress is saved in your own browser so you can stop and come back. On a shared computer, clear it when you are done — there is a button for that inside the tool.

Your SSN and DoD ID are never asked for.

Both forms want them in eight places, and every one is left blank for you to fill in by hand. Nothing here stores or transmits them, because nothing here ever sees them.

No tool can promise you a claim outcome, and this one does not try.

DD Form 2807-1 is a sworn statement carrying a federal false-statement penalty of up to five years’ confinement or a $10,000 fine. Every answer this tool proposes is shown to you with the record it came from, and nothing is written to a form until you confirm it. Read what you sign.
