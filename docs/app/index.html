About you

These are the things your medical records can't tell us. They go straight onto both forms — you're the source, so there's nothing to review later.

This all runs inside your browser tab.

Your records are read on this computer and never sent anywhere — there is no server to send them to. You can disconnect from the internet after this page loads and the whole thing still works.

Social Security Number and DoD ID are not collected.

They are intentionally left blank on both forms. Fill them in by hand in a PDF reader before you hand the package to your VSO.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back. They stay on this computer. On a shared computer, clear them when you're done.

This browser build reads PDFs that have a text layer — an MHS Genesis export does. It cannot OCR a scanned AF form, because that uses a native library the browser can't run. Scanned files will say so rather than silently producing nothing.

Read these carefully.

DD Form 2807-1 is an official statement carrying federal penalties for a false statement (up to 5 years confinement or a $10,000 fine). Your records may contain entries you've never seen. Confirm only what you know to be true.

Explain your “Yes” answers

DD Form 2807-1 requires every “Yes” to be explained in Item 29, and SHA Part A has an explanation box for each one. These drafts state only what your records document — edit them freely, in your own words.

These were generated from your records, so read them before they go on the form. They deliberately don't characterise severity, cause, or service connection — that's your and your VSO's judgement, not this tool's.

Then what?

Take this package to an accredited Veterans Service Officer. They're free, and they're accredited to advise on what to claim — this tool isn't and doesn't. VA's directory: va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or the Department of Defense. This tool organizes your own records. It does not file anything with VA and does not advise on what to claim — take the finished package to an accredited VSO.
