# Who can use this, in plain English

The formal terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE) — the PolyForm Noncommercial
License 1.0.0. This page says the same thing without the lawyering, because
the people this tool is for should not have to read a license to find out
whether they are allowed to use it.

## Free, forever, no permission needed

**If you are a veteran or service member using this for your own claim** —
it is yours. Use it, keep it, run it offline, copy it to a thumb drive, give
it to someone in your unit. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay, no
account, no email address.

**If you are an accredited Veterans Service Officer** — VFW, DAV, American
Legion, a state or county veterans office, or any other accredited
organization — use it with every veteran who walks in. That is a permitted
noncommercial purpose under the license, explicitly.

**If you are a nonprofit, a school, a clinic, a public health or public
safety organization, or any government body** — same answer. The license
names you specifically, and the source of your funding does not matter.

**If you want to read the code, fork it, fix it, or learn from it** — go
ahead. That is why it is public.

## What needs a separate license

**Selling it, or building a paid product or service on it.** That includes
charging veterans for claims help using this software, bundling it into a
commercial offering, or running it as a paid service.

That is not a technicality. Paid claim consultants are a known problem in
this space, and 38 U.S.C. § 5901 restricts who may charge for preparing or
presenting a VA claim at all. The license and the law point the same
direction here: this exists so veterans do not have to pay someone to fill
in a form.

If you have a commercial use in mind, see [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md).
