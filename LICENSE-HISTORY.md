# License history

This project changed license. Both states are recorded here because a
license change that is not written down is a license dispute waiting to
happen.

| Period | License |
|---|---|
| 21 August 2026 – 23 August 2026 | MIT |
| 24 August 2026 onward | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) |

## What that means

**The MIT grant on the earlier versions is irrevocable.** Anyone who
obtained a copy while the project was MIT-licensed keeps MIT rights to that
copy — including the right to use it commercially — and relicensing the
project does not reach back and change that. It never can, for any project.

In practice the exposure is small: the repository had no forks, no stars and
no watchers for the whole of that window. But "small" is not "none", and
pretending otherwise would be the kind of thing that surfaces later at the
worst possible moment.

**Everything from 24 August 2026 is PolyForm Noncommercial.** All the work
since that date — and there is a lot of it — is under the new terms.

## Why the change

The tool should be free for the people it was built for: veterans, service
members, and the accredited Veterans Service Officers who help them. MIT
gave them that, and gave everyone else the right to sell it too.

PolyForm Noncommercial keeps the first half and drops the second. See
[NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for who can use it without asking, which is almost
everybody who would want to.
