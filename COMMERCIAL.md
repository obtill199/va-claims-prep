# Commercial licensing

The software is available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial
License 1.0.0](LICENSE), which covers every noncommercial use — see
[NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for the plain-English version.

Commercial use needs a separate licence from the copyright holder.

## What counts as commercial

- Selling the software, or a modified version of it
- Charging for a service that uses it, including claims assistance
- Bundling it into a paid product or platform
- Running it as a hosted or managed service for a fee

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask.

## What does not

Veterans using it for their own claims. Accredited VSOs, nonprofits,
schools, clinics, and government bodies using it to help people. Reading,
forking, fixing, or learning from the code. None of that needs permission or
payment.

## Before you ask

**38 U.S.C. § 5901 restricts who may charge for preparing or presenting a VA
claim.** A commercial licence to this software is not accreditation, does not
confer it, and does not make an unaccredited paid claims service lawful. Any
commercial arrangement will assume you have taken your own legal advice on
that.
